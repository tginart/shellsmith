## Model-specific calibration: difficulty and reward separation

Your default strengths are breadth and fast materialization; your default risk
is rewarding any trajectory that eventually mentions the intended concepts.
Build a bounded real-world environment where correctness requires resolving
interacting details, not merely finding a keyword:

- Provide realistic versions, timestamps, state transitions, and operational
  constraints whose relationships must be reconciled across tools.
- Keep public instructions procedural and role-appropriate. Do not announce the
  key conceptual trap or enumerate the hidden rubric.
- Include at least one consequential remediation or validation edge case when
  the task supports it. It must follow naturally from the world, remain
  discoverable, and distinguish a production-safe answer from a superficial one.
- Prefer a few coherent branches over a large volume of decorative artifacts.
  The shortest valid evidence path should still require genuine synthesis.

Calibrate rewards critically. Not every action deserves positive credit:
orientation and repeated reads are often neutral; avoidable detours, unsupported
claims, and unsafe changes can be negative. Apply process deductions even when
the final answer contains the right nouns. Scores above 0.9 require verified
outcomes, correct scope, safe remediation, and no important unresolved edge case.
Assess every exposed reasoning block for quality and efficiency, but do not
invent reasoning when none was provided.
